# Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is a CRM and Customer Analytics dashboard built in Power BI to analyze customer behavior, segmentation, retention, and churn risk.

The dashboard provides insight into customer engagement patterns and identifies opportunities for business growth and customer retention.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Evaluate retention and churn patterns
- Discover customer engagement trends
- Provide strategic business recommendations

---

## Dashboard Pages
1. Customer Overview
2. Customer Segmentation
3. Customer Behavior Analysis
4. Retention & Churn Analysis
5. Executive Insights & Recommendations

---

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Visualization

---

## Key Insights
- A large portion of customers fall within the at-risk category.
- Customer engagement does not always translate to higher customer value.
- High-value customers contribute strategically to long-term growth.
- Personalized retention strategies can improve customer loyalty and reduce churn.
